A working library of 38 Grasshopper definitions collected over four years of academic, professional and research work. Parametric systems, form-finding, environmental analysis, optimization and infrastructure geometry.

Each file is a distilled workflow rather than a project script: open it, read the wires, adapt it. Nothing here is a fixed solution.

Shafiul Islam, architectural and computational designer. MS Design Technology, University of Washington.

Previews are schematic sketches drawn live in the browser to show the idea of each definition. They are not renders from the files. Open the .gh in Grasshopper to see the real thing.

Source and issues on GitHub. Shafiul Islam.
