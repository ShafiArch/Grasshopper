A working collection of Grasshopper definitions gathered from academic, professional and research work, kept for the parts that proved reusable. Parametric systems, form-finding, environmental analysis, optimization and infrastructure geometry.

Each file is a distilled workflow rather than a project script: open it, read the wires, adapt it.

Contributor: Shafiul Islam, MS Design Technology, University of Washington.

Previews are schematic sketches drawn live in the browser to show the idea of each definition. They are not renders from the files. Open the .gh in Grasshopper to see the real thing.
