Order the cards

Drag cards into the order you want them on the site, first at the top. The arrows do the same from the keyboard. Download the update and drag the two files into the repository. Nothing on this page touches the repository by itself.

    The update contains assets/site.js with the new order and an index.html with the cache number raised.

    1. Unzip the download.
    2. Open the repository on GitHub, click Add file, then Upload files, drag in index.html and the assets folder. Commit.
    3. Wait a couple of minutes, then refresh the site.
